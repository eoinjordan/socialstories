Social Stories & Care Pathways

Picture-led stories and step-by-step care pathways for people with literacy, communication or learning differences. One short sentence and one clear picture per screen, read aloud, and able to stay locked open on a tablet as a status radiator.

Build one here, free

The builder on this page needs no account and no internet after it has loaded. Stories are saved in this browser and can be exported as a file.

On a tablet

The Android app caches everything on the device, so it works with no signal at all, and can pin itself to the screen so a story cannot be swiped away.

Written to a published standard

The builder checks what you write against the Social Stories 10.4 criteria — the balance of describing to coaching sentences, second person, judging words, and the rule that at least half of the stories written for someone should celebrate what they already do well. It is a drafting aid, not a certification: most of the criteria are human work no checker can see.
